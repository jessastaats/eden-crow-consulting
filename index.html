About Eden & Crow

Business support without adding more management to your plate.

Eden & Crow Consulting was created around a simple idea: business owners should not have to become project managers every time something needs to get done.

We provide a managed approach to outsourced project work. Instead of handing you a list of freelancers or leaving you to coordinate multiple providers, we oversee the process from scope through delivery.

That means thoughtful planning, clear communication, resourceful problem solving, careful coordination, and a final quality review before the work reaches you. The goal is straightforward: give important projects the structure and attention they need without creating another management job for you.
